## 5 ways to pay less for IPTV

1. **Test first, then go long.** Use the free 3-hour trial (or one month) to confirm the service works on your internet, then switch to a 12- or 24-month plan for the lowest monthly cost.
2. **Buy only the screens you use.** Each extra simultaneous connection adds to the price. Count how many people really watch at the same time.
3. **Use a device you already own.** Most Smart TVs, Firesticks, phones and laptops work — there's nothing extra to buy. See [the best IPTV apps for Firestick](/blog/best-iptv-apps-firestick).
4. **Cancel the extras you no longer need.** Once you've switched, drop the cable package, box rental and add-on channel packs. Our [cord-cutting guide](/blog/cut-the-cord-switch-to-iptv) shows how.
5. **Time your purchase.** Start a longer plan just before the sports season you care about, so it covers kickoff through the playoffs.

## What do you lose with a cheap plan?

With Multivision IPTV, nothing — the 24-month plan has exactly the same channels, quality and support as the 1-month plan. The only difference is that you commit for longer, which is why we recommend testing first.

With other providers, an unusually low price can hide real costs:

- **Buffering at peak times**, because too many viewers share each server.
- **No working program guide**, so finding something to watch is a chore.
- **Slow or no support** when something breaks.
- **Services that vanish**, taking a prepaid year with them.

## Warning signs of a "too cheap" IPTV deal

- Lifetime plans or one-off prices that seem impossibly low.
- No free trial.
- No published terms, privacy policy or refund policy.
- Payment only through untraceable methods, with no receipt.
- A channel list full of duplicates and dead links.
