## Option 1: An over-the-air antenna

A digital antenna picks up local broadcast stations for free, and many big games air on them. The picture is uncompressed HD, so it often looks excellent. The limits: no national cable sports networks, reception depends on your distance from transmitters, and you can only watch on the TV the antenna is connected to.

## Option 2: Live TV streaming services

Live TV streaming services offer bundles of channels over the internet with no installation. They're convenient, but monthly prices have risen steadily, and rights are split — the game you want may be on a channel your plan doesn't include.

## Game-day checklist: no buffering at kickoff

1. **Plug in Ethernet**, or sit the router within clear range on 5 GHz Wi-Fi.
2. **Restart your streaming device** an hour before the game.
3. **Pause downloads and game updates** on other devices in the house.
4. **Open the channel early** so the stream settles before the start.
5. **Have a backup device ready** — your phone with the same app.
