**Quick answer:** IPTV buffers when your device can't get a fast, steady stream of data. The fixes that work most often are: **use Ethernet or 5 GHz Wi-Fi**, **restart your router and device**, **clear the app cache**, **close background apps and downloads**, and **lower the stream quality** if your connection is under 10 Mbps per HD stream (25 Mbps for 4K).

## Find the cause first

| Symptom | Most likely cause | Go to fix |
|---|---|---|
| Every channel buffers | Slow or unstable home connection | 1, 2, 3 |
| Only in the evening | Network congestion at peak times | 3, 7, 8 |
| Only one TV buffers | Weak Wi-Fi in that room or slow device | 1, 4, 5 |
| One channel buffers, others fine | That stream's source | Try another feed, or tell support |
| Picture freezes but audio continues | Device struggling to decode | 4, 6 |

## The 9 fixes

### 1. Use a wired Ethernet connection

The biggest single improvement. Wi-Fi drops packets when walls, distance and neighbors' networks interfere; a cable doesn't. Firesticks need an inexpensive Ethernet adapter; most Smart TVs and Android boxes have a port built in.

### 2. Switch to 5 GHz Wi-Fi

If you can't run a cable, connect to your router's 5 GHz network. It's faster and less crowded than 2.4 GHz, though it has shorter range — move the router closer or add a mesh point.

### 3. Restart your router and device

Unplug the router for 30 seconds, then restart your streaming device. This clears memory and refreshes the connection — it fixes more buffering than any setting.

### 4. Clear the app cache

- **Firestick:** Settings → Applications → Manage installed applications → your IPTV app → Clear cache.
- **Android TV:** Settings → Apps → your IPTV app → Clear cache.

### 5. Close background apps and free up storage

Streaming sticks have limited memory. Close apps you aren't using and keep at least 1 GB of storage free.

### 6. Lower the stream quality

If your speed is under 25 Mbps, choose HD instead of 4K. In most IPTV apps you can select a lower-quality version of a channel or change the player buffer size in settings.

### 7. Stop competing traffic

Game downloads, cloud backups and video calls on other devices compete for bandwidth. Pause them during live games, or enable Quality of Service (QoS) on your router to prioritize the TV.

### 8. Change your DNS

Switching your device or router to a fast public DNS service can speed up connections to streaming servers. Look for the DNS setting under your router's network settings.

### 9. Update your device and app

Outdated software can cause playback problems. Install system updates and the latest version of your IPTV app.
