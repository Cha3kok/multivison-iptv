## What You Need Before Starting

Before configuring your MAG box, make sure you have:

1. **Your MAG box and HDMI cable** (most boxes don't include this — buy separately)
2. **Ethernet cable or Wi-Fi credentials** (Ethernet is strongly recommended for stability)
3. **Your IPTV portal URL** — provided by your UK IPTV provider (Multivision IPTV sends this after purchase)
4. **Your MAG box MAC address** — printed on the sticker underneath the device

The MAC address is critical. Your IPTV provider uses it to authorise your specific device — you must provide this to Multivision IPTV when you subscribe.

## Step-by-Step MAG Box Setup for UK IPTV

### Step 1 — Connect Your MAG Box

1. Connect the HDMI cable from the MAG box to your TV
2. Connect an Ethernet cable from your router to the MAG box (recommended), or note down your Wi-Fi details
3. Connect the power adapter and turn the device on
4. Your TV should display the MAG box home screen

### Step 2 — Configure Network

**For Ethernet users:** The MAG box will connect automatically — no configuration needed.

**For Wi-Fi users:**
1. From the MAG box home screen, press **Menu** on the remote
2. Go to **System Settings** → **Network** → **Wi-Fi**
3. Select your Wi-Fi network and enter the password
4. Save and wait for connection confirmation

### Step 3 — Enter Your Portal URL

This is the most important step:

1. Press **Menu** on the remote
2. Go to **System Settings** → **Servers**
3. Select **Portals**
4. In **Portal 1 URL**, enter the portal URL provided by your IPTV provider
   - Example format: `http://yourprovider.com/c/`
5. Leave Portal 1 Name as "Portal 1" or rename it
6. Press **OK** and then **Restart Portal** to apply

### Step 4 — Load Your Channels

After the portal restarts, your IPTV service will load automatically. You'll see the EPG and channel list populate within 30–60 seconds depending on your internet speed.

## Troubleshooting Common MAG Box Issues

### "Portal Not Found" or "Server Unreachable"

1. Double-check the portal URL — a single typo prevents connection
2. Confirm your internet connection is active (try loading a streaming site on another device)
3. Contact your IPTV provider to verify the portal URL hasn't changed
4. Try restarting the MAG box and re-entering the portal

### Buffering During Live Channels

1. Switch from Wi-Fi to Ethernet — wireless connections cause most buffering issues
2. Restart your router
3. Check if other devices are consuming heavy bandwidth (downloads, video calls)
4. Contact your IPTV provider — peak hours occasionally require server switching

### MAG Box Freezing or Crashing

1. Check your MAG box has adequate ventilation — place it on a hard, flat surface with space around it
2. Factory reset: Menu → System Settings → Reset Settings
3. Update firmware: Menu → System Settings → Software Update
