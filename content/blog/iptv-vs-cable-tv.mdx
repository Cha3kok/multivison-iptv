**Quick answer:** **IPTV is cheaper and more flexible than cable** — no contract, no box rental, thousands more channels and it works on every screen. **Cable is more reliable on a weak internet connection**, because it doesn't use your broadband. If you have at least 10 Mbps per HD stream, IPTV is the better value for most US households.

## Channels and content

Cable packages are tiered: the channels you want are often in a higher tier or a paid add-on. IPTV services typically include everything in every plan — Multivision IPTV includes 50,000+ live channels, 200,000+ on-demand titles and international TV in Spanish and many other languages on every plan.

## Picture quality

Many cable channels are still sent in 720p or 1080i to save bandwidth. IPTV streams can reach 1080p and 4K when the source is produced that way and your connection has about 25 Mbps per 4K stream.

## Reliability

This is cable's real advantage. Cable TV works even when your internet doesn't, and it doesn't slow down when someone else in the house is downloading. IPTV depends on your connection, so a wired Ethernet link or strong 5 GHz Wi-Fi makes a big difference — see our [buffering fixes](/blog/iptv-buffering-fix-guide).

## Who should choose which?

**Choose IPTV if you…**

- want to cut your monthly TV cost substantially;
- watch on phones, tablets or several TVs;
- want international or Spanish-language channels;
- don't want a contract or equipment rental.

**Stick with cable if you…**

- have slow, capped or unreliable internet;
- are locked into a contract with a large termination fee (wait until it ends).
