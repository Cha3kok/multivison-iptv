**Quick answer:** To watch IPTV on an iPhone or iPad, install an IPTV player from the App Store — such as **GSE Smart IPTV**, **IPTVX** or **Smarters Player Lite** — then add your provider's **Xtream Codes** login or **M3U link**. Your channels load in seconds, and you can send them to your TV with **AirPlay**. Setup takes about 3 minutes.

**What you need**

- An iPhone or iPad on a recent version of iOS or iPadOS.
- Wi-Fi or mobile data (about 10 Mbps for HD).
- An IPTV subscription — for example a [Multivision IPTV](/) plan or free trial.

## How to set up IPTV on iPhone

1. Open the **App Store** and install your chosen IPTV player.
2. Open the app and allow it to access your local network if asked (needed for casting).
3. Tap **Add playlist** (or **+**).
4. Choose **Xtream Codes API** and enter the **server address**, **username** and **password** from your provider — or choose **M3U URL** and paste your link.
5. Tap **Save** or **Add user**. Live TV, movies and series appear in a few seconds.

**Tip:** copy your login from WhatsApp or email and paste it — typing long server addresses on a phone is the most common cause of "invalid login" errors.

## How to watch on your TV with AirPlay

1. Connect your iPhone and your Apple TV or AirPlay-compatible Smart TV to the **same Wi-Fi network**.
2. Start a channel in your IPTV app.
3. Tap the **AirPlay** icon in the player (a rectangle with a triangle).
4. Choose your TV.

If your TV isn't listed, check that AirPlay is turned on in the TV's settings. For the best long-term experience on a big screen, a dedicated device is smoother — see [IPTV on a Smart TV](/blog/iptv-smart-tv-setup).

## How much data does IPTV use on a phone?

| Quality | Approximate data per hour |
|---|---|
| SD | 0.7 GB |
| HD | 1.5–3 GB |
| 4K | 7 GB or more |

Use Wi-Fi for long sessions, and lower the stream quality in the app settings when you're on mobile data.

## Troubleshooting on iPhone and iPad

- **"Invalid login":** paste the details instead of typing; include `http://` and the port number.
- **No sound:** check the silent switch and the in-app audio track setting.
- **AirPlay drops:** keep both devices on the same 5 GHz network and close other apps.
- **Buffering:** switch from mobile data to Wi-Fi, or see our [buffering fixes](/blog/iptv-buffering-fix-guide).
