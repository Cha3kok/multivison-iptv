**Quick answer:** To set up IPTV on Android TV or Google TV, open the **Google Play Store**, install an IPTV player such as **TiviMate** or **IPTV Smarters Pro**, choose **Add playlist**, and enter the **Xtream Codes** login or **M3U link** from your provider. Your channels and program guide load in a minute or two. The whole process takes about 5 minutes.

**What you need**

- An Android TV or Google TV device (TV, box or streamer) connected to the internet.
- A Google account signed in on the device.
- An IPTV subscription — for example a [Multivision IPTV](/) plan or free 3-hour trial.

## Step 1: Check your connection and update the device

1. Connect via **Ethernet** if possible; otherwise use **5 GHz Wi-Fi**.
2. Go to **Settings → System → About → System update** and install any updates.
3. Make sure you have at least 1 GB of free storage (**Settings → Apps** shows what's using space).

## Step 2: Install an IPTV player

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your TV.
2. Search for **TiviMate** or **IPTV Smarters Pro**.
3. Select **Install** and wait for it to finish.

| App | Best for | Login types |
|---|---|---|
| TiviMate | Cable-style guide, recording (premium) | Xtream Codes, M3U |
| IPTV Smarters Pro | Simple menus for beginners | Xtream Codes, M3U |
| Televizo | Clean free interface | Xtream Codes, M3U |

## Step 3: Add your IPTV subscription

### Using Xtream Codes (recommended)

1. Open the app and choose **Add playlist**.
2. Select **Xtream Codes**.
3. Enter the **server address**, **username** and **password** from your welcome message.
4. Select **Next**. The app loads your live channels, movies and series.

Xtream Codes is easier to type on a remote and loads the program guide automatically in most apps.

### Using an M3U link

1. Choose **Add playlist → M3U playlist**.
2. Enter the playlist URL exactly as provided (tip: send it to yourself and copy it with the phone remote app to avoid typos).
3. If the guide doesn't load automatically, add the **EPG URL** from your provider in the playlist settings.

## Step 4: Set up the guide and favorites

- **Refresh the EPG** once the playlist loads, and set it to update every day.
- **Hide channel groups** you never watch to make the list faster to browse.
- **Add favorites** — in most apps, long-press a channel and choose *Add to favorites*.
- **Turn on catch-up** in the app settings to replay programs from the last 7 days on supported channels.

## Common Android TV setup problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Invalid login" | Re-type the server address including `http://` and the port number; check for spaces. |
| Channels load but no guide | Refresh the EPG in the playlist settings; wait a few minutes on first load. |
| App is slow to open | Clear the app cache (**Settings → Apps → [app] → Clear cache**) and restart the device. |
| Streams buffer | Switch to Ethernet or 5 GHz Wi-Fi; see our [buffering fixes](/blog/iptv-buffering-fix-guide). |
