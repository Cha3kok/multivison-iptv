**Quick answer:** To set up IPTV on a Smart TV, open your TV's app store, install an IPTV player that supports **Xtream Codes** or **M3U** playlists, and enter the login from your provider. Sony, TCL and Hisense TVs running **Google TV** can use TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro from the Play Store. **Samsung (Tizen)** and **LG (webOS)** TVs have their own IPTV apps, which vary by model — if yours has none, a Firestick is the easy fix.

## Step 1: Find out which system your TV runs

| TV brand | Operating system | Where to get IPTV apps |
|---|---|---|
| Samsung | Tizen | Samsung Apps |
| LG | webOS | LG Content Store |
| Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips (many models) | Google TV / Android TV | Google Play Store |
| TCL, Hisense, Roku-branded TVs | Roku OS | Roku Channel Store (limited IPTV support) |
| Some Hisense models | VIDAA | VIDAA app store (limited) |

Check **Settings → About** (or **Support → About this TV**) if you're not sure.

## Step 2: Set up IPTV on your TV

### Google TV / Android TV (Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips)

1. Open the **Google Play Store**.
2. Install **TiviMate** or **IPTV Smarters Pro**.
3. Choose **Add playlist → Xtream Codes** and enter your server, username and password.
4. Wait for channels and the guide to load.

Full walkthrough: [IPTV on Android TV and Google TV](/blog/iptv-android-tv-setup).

### Samsung (Tizen)

1. Press **Home** and open **Apps**.
2. Search for an IPTV player that supports Xtream Codes or M3U playlists.
3. Install it and open it. Some apps show a **device ID** and ask you to add your playlist on their website; others let you type the login on the TV.
4. Enter your login details and restart the app.

### LG (webOS)

1. Press **Home** and open the **LG Content Store**.
2. Search for an IPTV player that supports Xtream Codes or M3U.
3. Install and open it, then add your playlist on the TV or through the app's website using the device ID shown.

Some Samsung and LG IPTV apps are free; others charge a small one-time activation fee after a trial period. App availability changes, so check reviews in your TV's store before paying.

### Roku and VIDAA TVs

These systems have very limited IPTV app support. The simplest solution is to plug a **Firestick** or **Google TV streamer** into a spare HDMI port — see [the best IPTV apps for Firestick](/blog/best-iptv-apps-firestick).

## Step 3: Get the best picture

- **Use Ethernet.** Most Smart TVs have an Ethernet port, and a wired connection is the most stable option for live sports.
- **Match the picture mode.** Use *Standard* or *Movie* mode and turn off motion smoothing if sports look unnatural.
- **Check your speed.** Allow 10 Mbps for HD and 25 Mbps for 4K per stream.
- **Keep the TV updated** under *Settings → Support → Software update*.

| | Smart TV app | Firestick / Google TV streamer |
|---|---|---|
| Extra cost | None | One-time device purchase |
| App choice | Limited on Tizen/webOS | Wide |
| Speed | Depends on the TV's processor | Usually faster |
| Catch-up and recording | App-dependent | Widely supported |

## Troubleshooting

- **App not in store:** your TV model or region may not support it — use a streaming stick.
- **Login rejected:** re-check the server address, including `http://` and the port number.
- **No guide:** refresh the EPG in the app's playlist settings.
- **Buffering:** follow our [IPTV buffering fixes](/blog/iptv-buffering-fix-guide).
