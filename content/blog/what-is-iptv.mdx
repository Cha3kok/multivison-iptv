**Quick answer:** IPTV (internet protocol television) is live TV and on-demand video delivered over the internet instead of through a cable line or satellite dish. You watch it through an app on a Smart TV, Firestick, phone or computer. In the USA, an IPTV service like [Multivision IPTV](/) costs from $5.42 to $19.99 a month with no contract.

**Key takeaways**

- IPTV uses your home internet connection — no cable box, dish or installer.
- You watch through a free player app on a device you already own.
- It offers live channels, a program guide, catch-up TV and on-demand movies.
- It needs a steady connection: about 10 Mbps per HD stream.

## How does IPTV work?

Traditional cable sends every channel down a coaxial line at the same time, and your cable box tunes into one. IPTV works differently: when you pick a channel, your device requests that one stream from a server, which sends it to you over the internet as data packets — the same way a web page or video call reaches you.

Because only the channel you're watching is sent, IPTV can offer far more channels than a cable line has room for, and the same subscription works on any internet-connected screen.

In practice, using IPTV looks like this:

1. You subscribe and receive a login — an **M3U playlist link** or an **Xtream Codes** server, username and password.
2. You install an **IPTV player app** such as TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro or GSE IPTV.
3. You enter the login, and the app loads your channel list, program guide and on-demand library.

IPTV services typically offer three kinds of content:

## What do you need to watch IPTV?

- **An internet connection.** Allow 10 Mbps per HD stream and 25 Mbps per 4K stream.
- **A compatible device.** Firestick or Fire TV, a Smart TV, an Android TV box, an iPhone or iPad, a MAG box, or a computer.
- **An IPTV player app.** Most are free; some charge a small one-time fee for premium features.
- **An IPTV subscription** that provides the channels.

## Is IPTV legal in the USA?

IPTV is a delivery technology, and the technology itself is legal — several mainstream live TV services in the US are IPTV services. What determines whether a particular service is legal is whether it has the rights to the content it distributes. Before subscribing to any provider, read its terms of service and copyright policy. ## Pros and cons of IPTV

**Pros**

- Far more channels than a typical cable package, including international TV.
- Works on every screen in the house and when you travel.
- No contract, no installation and no equipment rental.
- Usually much cheaper than cable.

**Cons**

- Depends on your internet connection — a slow or busy network causes buffering.
- Setup involves entering a login into an app (it takes about 5 minutes).
- Quality and reliability vary a lot between providers, so test before you commit.
