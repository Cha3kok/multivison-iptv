## What is an IPTV subscription?

IPTV stands for *internet protocol television*. Instead of receiving channels through a coaxial cable or a satellite dish, your TV, phone or streaming stick receives them as a data stream over your home internet.

An IPTV subscription is the access you buy to that stream. After you subscribe, the provider sends you login details — usually an **M3U playlist link** or an **Xtream Codes login** (a server address, username and password). You enter those details into a free IPTV player app and your channel list loads in seconds.

## How many devices can use one subscription?

Each plan supports **1, 2, 3 or 4 simultaneous connections** — you choose when you subscribe. A "connection" is one stream playing at one time, so a household of four watching on four different screens needs four connections. You can install the app on as many devices as you like; only the number playing at once matters.

## What internet speed do you need?

| Picture quality | Minimum speed per stream |
|---|---|
| SD | 5 Mbps |

If you mainly watch live TV and sports, an IPTV subscription usually costs a fraction of a cable package and works on every screen in the house, not just the one with the cable box.
