**Quick answer:** To set up IPTV on a MAG box, send your provider the box's **MAC address** (on the sticker underneath, usually starting with `00:1A:79`), then go to **Settings → Servers → Portals**, enter the **portal URL** they give you, save, and **restart** the box. The portal loads your channels automatically — no username, password or app needed.

**What you need**

- A MAG box connected to your TV (HDMI) and to the internet (Ethernet recommended).
- Your box's MAC address.
- A portal URL from your IPTV provider — [Multivision IPTV](/) sends one within minutes of activation.

## Step 1: Find your MAC address

Look for a sticker on the bottom or back of the box. The MAC address is a 12-character code in the format `00:1A:79:XX:XX:XX`. You can also find it on screen under **Settings → System information** (sometimes called *Device info*).

Send this exact code to your provider. The portal is linked to your MAC address, so a single wrong character will stop it working.

## Step 2: Add the portal URL

1. Turn on the box and wait for the main screen.
2. Open **Settings** (the gear icon) — on some models press **SETUP/SET** on the remote.
3. Select **System settings → Servers → Portals**.
4. In **Portal 1 name**, type a name such as *Multivision*.
5. In **Portal 1 URL**, enter the portal address from your provider exactly as written.
6. Select **OK** to save.

## Step 3: Restart and load the portal

1. Go back to **System settings** and choose **Restart portal** (or unplug the box for 10 seconds).
2. The box connects to the portal and loads your channel list and program guide.
3. Press **TV** or open the channel list to start watching.

The first load can take a minute while the guide downloads.

## Step 4: Useful MAG settings

- **Video → Output resolution**: match your TV (1080p or 4K on 4K-capable models).
- **Network → Wired (Ethernet)**: use a cable for the most stable picture.
- **Favorites**: highlight a channel and press **F** (or the favorites key) to add it.
- **Software update**: **Settings → Software update** keeps the box compatible with newer portals.

## Common MAG box errors and fixes

| Error | What it means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| STB blocked | MAC not registered or plan expired | Confirm the MAC address and plan status with your provider |
| Loading portal… (stuck) | Wrong URL or no internet | Re-enter the portal URL; check the network cable |
| Authorization failed | MAC mismatch | Send the MAC from the sticker, not from memory |
| No channels | Portal loaded but list empty | Restart the portal; ask support to resync your MAC |
| Buffering | Network congestion | Use Ethernet; see [IPTV buffering fixes](/blog/iptv-buffering-fix-guide) |

## MAG box vs app-based devices

MAG boxes are simple — no apps, no logins, just a portal — which makes them great for less tech-savvy family members. App-based devices such as a Firestick or Android TV box offer more apps, catch-up features and on-demand libraries.
