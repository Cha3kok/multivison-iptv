**Quick answer:** On-demand **streaming apps** are best for watching movies and original series whenever you like. **IPTV** is best for **live TV** — sports, news and thousands of channels as they air — and it includes an on-demand library and catch-up as well. Many US households now use IPTV for everything live and keep at most one on-demand app for the originals they love.

**Key takeaways**

- Streaming apps = on-demand libraries; IPTV = live channels plus on-demand.
- Live sports and news are where IPTV clearly wins.
- Several separate subscriptions often cost more than one IPTV plan.
- Both work on the same devices, so you can mix and match.

## Where streaming apps win

- **Exclusive original series and films** you can't get anywhere else.
- **Very simple setup** — install the app and sign in.
- **Offline downloads** for flights and commutes.

## Where IPTV wins

- **Live TV of every kind** — sports, breaking news, kids' channels and entertainment as they air.
- **Breadth** — 50,000+ channels including Spanish-language and other international TV.
- **One payment** instead of juggling several monthly bills.
- **Catch-up TV** — replay the last 7 days on supported channels.

## The best of both: a common combination

A popular setup for US households:

1. **IPTV for everything live** — sports, news, kids and international channels.
2. **One on-demand app** for a specific original series, subscribed only while it's airing.
3. **Cancel and rejoin** on-demand apps season by season instead of paying for all of them year-round.

Both run side by side on the same Firestick or Smart TV — see [the best IPTV apps for Firestick](/blog/best-iptv-apps-firestick).

## Which should you choose?

- **Choose IPTV** if you watch live sports, news or international TV, or want one bill for the whole household.
- **Choose an on-demand app** if you mostly binge specific original shows.
- **Combine them** if you want live TV plus a particular exclusive series.
