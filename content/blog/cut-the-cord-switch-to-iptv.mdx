**Quick answer:** To cut the cord and switch to IPTV, (1) test an IPTV service on your own TV and internet with a free trial, (2) confirm you have about 10 Mbps per HD stream, (3) set up every TV and phone in the house, (4) run IPTV alongside cable for a week or two, then (5) cancel the TV part of your cable bill, keep internet only, and return all equipment.

**Key takeaways**

- Never cancel cable before testing IPTV on your own connection.
- Keep your internet service — IPTV runs on it.
- Return every box and remote to avoid equipment fees.
- Check for contract early-termination fees before you call.

## Step 1: List what you actually watch

Write down the shows, sports and channels each person in the house watches in a typical week, and how many people watch at the same time. That tells you which features matter and how many simultaneous screens you need.

## Step 2: Test IPTV first

Start a free trial and use it the way you'll really use it — on the main TV, in the evening, during a live game. Check that the channels you need are there and that the program guide works. [Multivision IPTV](/) offers a free 3-hour trial with no credit card.

## Step 3: Check your internet

| Household | Suggested download speed |
|---|---|
| 1 screen in HD | 10 Mbps |
| 2–3 screens in HD | 25–35 Mbps |
| 4 screens, some in 4K | 75 Mbps or more |

Run a speed test on the TV itself (not just your phone), in the evening. If speeds are low, a wired Ethernet connection or a better router placement usually helps more than upgrading your plan.

## Step 5: Run both side by side

Keep cable for a week or two while the household uses IPTV day to day. Add favorites, set up the guide, and show everyone how to use catch-up TV. Once nobody reaches for the cable remote, you're ready.

## Step 7: Cancel cable the right way

1. **Check your contract** for an end date and early-termination fee.
2. **Call or chat with your provider** and ask to remove TV service and keep internet only.
3. **Ask about the new internet-only price**, since bundle discounts often disappear.
4. **Decline retention offers** unless they genuinely beat your new setup.
5. **Get a confirmation number** and the date TV billing stops.

## Step 8: Return your equipment

Return every cable box, DVR and remote, and keep the receipt or tracking number. Unreturned equipment is one of the most common surprise charges after canceling.
